05 · ACADEMIC WORK

Projects

01
ACADEMIC PROJECT

Portable Spirometer for the Detection of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease and Asthma

Designed a low-cost, portable spirometer using ESP32 and pressure sensors to measure lung function parameters such as FEV1, FVC, and PEFR. The system integrates machine learning for COPD and asthma prediction, real-time monitoring through a Flask web dashboard, MySQL data storage, and downloadable PDF reports. Aimed at improving respiratory healthcare accessibility in rural and remote areas.

02
ACADEMIC PROJECT

Easy Wearable Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation (TENS)

Designed a compact, lightweight wearable TENS device for non-invasive pain management and muscle rehabilitation. The system uses controlled electrical impulses to stimulate sensory nerves and reduce pain. It integrates adjustable stimulation intensity, multiple waveforms, wireless connectivity, and mobile app-based control. Aimed at providing convenient, personalized pain relief for home and clinical use.
